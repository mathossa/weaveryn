# Weaveryn Rulesets

> **Status: Under development.**

This document will describe the architecture and lifecycle of World rules, Campaign
Rulesets, RulesetVersions, definitions, derived/customized Rulesets, and explicit
migrations.

> [!NOTE]
> **[Vision 2.0](VISION_2-0.md#12-rulesets-one-active-ruleset-per-campaign)** now
> defines the product direction where older material was incomplete: a Campaign has
> one active Ruleset; changing a shared/default Ruleset through Campaign rulings
> creates a Campaign-specific derived/customized Ruleset; and Ruleset changes use an
> assisted conversion workflow that preserves old mechanical state, with the player
> proposing character conversion and the DM approving it.

Until this document is expanded, `ARCHITECTURE.md` is authoritative for current
domain rules and `MVP.md` determines whether Ruleset functionality is currently in
scope. Vision 2.0 defines the long-term product behavior.
